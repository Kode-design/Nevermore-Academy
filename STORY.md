# Wednesday: The Nightshade Secret - Story Bible

## Overview
**Title:** Wednesday: The Nightshade Secret
**Genre:** RPG / Mystery / Supernatural
**Setting:** Nevermore Academy, Jericho, and surrounding supernatural locales.
**Protagonist:** Wednesday Addams (Primary), Thing (Support/Stealth).
**Duration:** 2+ Hours of gameplay.

## Prologue: The Diner
Wednesday is working at the Weathervane Cafe to "observe the chaotic interactions of the lower species" (and conduct research on a local legend).
She encounters the "Moonless Pack," a biker gang of werewolves.
**Key Event:** She spots the "Shadow Rider," a biker who never removes his helmet and is constantly shrouded in unnatural darkness.
The Rider leaves a burnt note warning her to "Run." Wednesday, naturally, decides to pursue him instead.

## Chapter 1: The Shadow's Trail
Wednesday tracks the biker gang back to their hideout in the abandoned Jericho train tunnels.
**Gameplay:**
- Investigation of the diner parking lot for clues (oil spills that smell like sulfur).
- Stealth section using Thing to infiltrate the vents of the werewolf clubhouse.
**Discovery:** The Shadow Rider isn't a werewolf. The "Shadow" is a parasitic entity called a "Gloom-Wraith," ancient and hungry. The Rider is its host—or its warden.

## Chapter 2: Return to Nevermore
Wednesday returns to Nevermore to consult the library's restricted section (The Nightshade Library).
**Characters:**
- **Enid Sinclair:** Provides emotional support and commentary on the "cute" bikers.
- **Xavier Thorpe:** Artist who has been drawing visions of the shadowy figure.
**Conflict:** Principal Weems has increased security. Wednesday must solve puzzles to unlock the secret entrance behind the statue of Edgar Allan Poe.
**Lore:** The Gloom-Wraith feeds on psychic distress. It was sealed away by Goody Addams centuries ago.

## Chapter 3: The Crypt of Crackstone
The research points to the old crypts. The Shadow Rider is trying to break the seal on an even greater evil, or so it seems.
**Twist:** The Shadow Rider is actually a descendant of Joseph Crackstone, trying to *contain* the Wraith, but his will is failing.
**Boss Battle:** A possessed gargoyle guarding the crypt entrance.

## Chapter 4: The Mindscape
Wednesday is pulled into a vision/mindscape by the Wraith.
**Gameplay:** Surreal, pixel-distorted levels representing Wednesday's own fears (which she claims not to have, but they manifest as "suffocating color" and "mandatory hugging").
She must find the mental anchor to escape.

## Chapter 5: The Night of the Blood Moon
The Wraith fully manifests, separating from the Rider. It intends to consume the psychic energy of the entire school during the upcoming Blood Moon festival.
**Final Battle:**
- Wednesday uses her cello to channel a sonic frequency that disrupts the Wraith.
- The player must switch between Wednesday (combat/distraction) and Thing (sabotaging the Wraith's tethers to the physical world).

## Epilogue
The Wraith is resealed into a talisman. Wednesday keeps it ("It makes a lovely paperweight").
The Shadow Rider rides off, helmet still on, but gives a nod of respect.
Wednesday returns to her novel, typing: "The darkness is not something to be outrun. It is something to be ridden."

## Side Quests
1. **The Siren's Song:** Help Bianca recover a lost amulet that controls her siren voice power.
2. **Bees in the Trap:** Eugene needs help collecting rare pollen from a carnivorous plant garden.
3. **Thing's Day Out:** A mini-adventure where Thing must navigate the boys' dorm to steal a specific book without being seen.

## Mechanics
- **Dual Character System:** Press 'T' to deploy Thing for small spaces and eavesdropping.
- **Deduction Board:** Combine clues found in the world to unlock new dialogue options and locations.
- **Psychic Visions:** randomly triggered quick-time events that give hints about the future or past.
