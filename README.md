# THE HORIZON VIEW APARTMENTS (THE BUILDING)

> *Ironic name: nothing can be seen from here.*

**A 2D Narrative Side-Scroller**

## The Building
**Location:** Edge of an overpopulated city.
**Structure:**
*   **Floor 1:** Apartment 1 (Reyes family), Apartment 2 (Lopez family)
*   **Floor 2:** Apartment 3 (Calder), Apartment 4 (Lyra & her cat), Apartment 5 (Rhea)

The building is old; it groans when the wind blows. The stairwell light flickers like it’s afraid of the dark. Roaches retreat only when bribed with poison. But within these walls, five tangled lives unfold.

---

## Characters

### 🧍‍♂️ APARTMENT 1 — KHRISTOPHER REYES
*   **Age:** 30
*   **Role:** Night-shift security, Family protector
*   **Personality:** Withdrawn, private, observant, intimidating exterior / gentle interior.
*   **Pet:** Cassiopeia (“Cass”), a tiny black & white rabbit.
*   **Core Arc:** Shame vs healing, Unexpected friendship, Rediscovering courage.

### 🧍‍♀️ APARTMENT 2 — MARCIA LOPEZ
*   **Age:** 22
*   **Personality:** Literal, socially blunt, hyper-observant, unintentionally hilarious.
*   **Habits:** Writes “Observation Logs”, hums classical tunes off-key.
*   **Core Arc:** Understanding vs misunderstanding, Independence, First friendship.

### 🧍‍♂️ APARTMENT 3 — CALDER “CAL” MENDEZ
*   **Age:** 27
*   **Role:** Barista, recovering addict (7 months clean).
*   **Secret:** His old dealer, Drexton, is back.
*   **Core Arc:** Temptation vs resilience, Found support network, Redemption.

### 🎸 APARTMENT 4 — LYRA HAN
*   **Age:** 25
*   **Role:** Busker + dishwasher.
*   **Secret:** Being pressured by a predatory producer, Vance.
*   **Core Arc:** Artistic integrity, Vulnerability vs confidence.

### 💋 APARTMENT 5 — RHEA
*   **Age:** 29
*   **Role:** Escort.
*   **Secret:** Stalked by a client named Harlow.
*   **Core Arc:** Survival, Boundaries, Hidden strength.

---

## Story Overview

The game explores the intersecting lives of these five residents. It begins with Khristopher answering (or ignoring) his door to meet Marcia, and expands into a web of interactions, friendships, and dangers.

### Act I Highlights
*   **Khristopher & Marcia:** A friendship formed over a rabbit.
*   **Calder & Drexton:** The threat of the past returning.
*   **Lyra:** Struggles with her music and Vance.
*   **Rhea:** The creeping danger of her stalker.
*   **Finale:** "The Night The Building Stopped Breathing" - A blackout traps them all together with their demons.

---

## Gameplay
*   **Side-Scroller:** Explore the apartment complex, move between floors and rooms.
*   **Dialogue System:** Branching conversations with choices that affect relationships and story outcomes.
*   **Atmosphere:** Focus on the sounds of the building, the lighting, and the emotional states of the characters.
